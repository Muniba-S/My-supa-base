    Our Story

    A platform, not just a shop

    Bari Imam is a community on the edge of Islamabad, known for its shrine and, quietly, for the needlework skill of its women — taught mother to daughter, rarely sold beyond the neighborhood.

    Sundrop exists to change that. We give each woman a storefront, a fair price for her work, and credit under her own name — turning a skill she already has into an income she controls.
